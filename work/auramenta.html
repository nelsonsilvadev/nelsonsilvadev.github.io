Auramenta

I built this private journal and mood tracker with React Native, Expo and TypeScript. It's available on {{ site.auramenta.platforms }}.

View on the App Store ↗ View on Google Play ↗ Visit website ↗

What I built

Journaling and local data

Journal entries with photos and voice notes, SQLite storage, mood tracking and goals. Text analysis happens on the device.

Backup and subscriptions

Auramenta+ includes optional encrypted backup and restore through iCloud on Apple devices and Google Drive on Android. I used RevenueCat for subscriptions and purchase restoration.

The app interface

Reusable components, navigation and animations built with Redux Toolkit, React Navigation and Reanimated.

Testing and releases

Automated tests with Jest and React Native Testing Library. I used EAS Build for App Store and Google Play releases, and EAS Update to deliver updates.
